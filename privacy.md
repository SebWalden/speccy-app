# Speccy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

Speccy does not collect, store or share any personal information.

**Your data stays on your device.** The players, games and stats you enter are saved only on your iPhone. Speccy has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

**Sharing is up to you.** When you share a game, club file, backup or card, it goes only where you send it (for example Messages, AirDrop, Mail, Files or Photos), using Apple's standard share sheet. Speccy never sends anything anywhere on its own.

**Photos.** If you save a card to Photos, Speccy asks for permission to add images to your library. It cannot see or read your existing photos.

**Children.** Speccy is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

**Deleting your data.** Delete players and games in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
