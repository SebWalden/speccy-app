# Speccy

Footy stats from the sideline. Speccy is an iPhone app for tracking a player's stats in community Australian rules football, live or after the game.

## Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **sebastiangwalden@gmail.com** and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

**Where is my data?** Only on your iPhone. Make a backup in Settings under Backup, and use Restore backup on a new phone.

**How do I share a game?** Open the game and tap Send Game. The other person opens the file in Speccy.

**Why don't my Other Games change my averages?** Games added as a count only have games and votes, so they count towards games played and votes but not other stats.

## Privacy

Speccy collects no data. Read the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
